Design mock — AUN-QA evidence page

A proposal for /teaching-learning/[programId], using this programme's real content. Nothing here is built yet.

  • Three levels, visibly different. Today a criterion heading and a sub-criterion heading are almost the same weight, so the page reads as one flat column. Here a criterion is a banded header with a rule under it, sub-criteria hang off a vertical spine with numbered markers, and evidence cards are deliberately the quietest layer — the reverse of today, where cards shout as loudly as the headings above them.
  • You can get to Criterion 5. The page is ~8,900px tall with no way to navigate it. A sticky rail lists every criterion with its coverage, marks the one you are reading, and includes the two criteria that hold nothing — so their absence is visible rather than discovered at the bottom.
  • Caveats stop competing with data. The measurement notes are worth keeping but not worth reading five times; they fold into a disclosure that stays closed until asked.
  • Two small SVGs only — a coverage ring per criterion and a 1–5 scale bar for evaluation means. No chrome for its own sake.
