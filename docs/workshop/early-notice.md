# Early notice: start this week

> **ภาษาไทย:** มีฉบับภาษาไทยของหน้านี้ที่ [`early-notice.th.md`](./early-notice.th.md)

Your team is confirmed for the AIUI workshop on **23–24 November**. The IT staff
attend both days; your executive joins on the second day. Before that there is a
**pre-workshop online on 16 November**, and a **readiness form due 19 November,
18:00**.

At the end of the workshop each team shows the Program Deep-Dive of **one of your
own programmes** running on your laptop with data. If time allows, you also show
an extra analysis you built yourselves.

Some of the preparation depends on other offices, so start it this week.

## This week

1. **Pick one programme.** Choose one your executive cares about and whose
   curriculum PDF you can get.
2. **Ask the data owners now** (registrar, human resources, finance, the unit
   that runs the graduate survey) for that programme's exports and its current
   curriculum PDF. The datasets and their columns are listed in
   [Importing from spreadsheets](../data-import.md). Requests to other offices
   can take days.
3. **Check your laptop.** It needs 16 GB of memory, 25 GB of free disk, and the
   right to install WSL2 and Docker. University-managed laptops often block them;
   see [Windows setup with WSL2](../guides/windows-wsl2.md). If yours does,
   arrange another laptop now.

You can start installing straight away with [Before the day](./00-before-the-day.md).

## Pick your data path

| Path | What you bring |
|---|---|
| **A. Real** | Your own exports for the programme, personal data removed, and its curriculum PDF. |
| **B. Mixed** | The exports you could get. The generator fills in the rest. |
| **C. Mock** | Only the curriculum PDF. The generator writes every file for your own university. |

All three paths reach the same demo. Choose C if you cannot get data in time:
it is a complete path, not a failure. The generator is described in
[Session 3](./03-university-data.md#no-exports-generate-a-mock-set-for-your-university).
At the demo you will say which parts of your data are real and which are mock.

## Personal data

Remove personal data before any file leaves your office:

- no national ID numbers or passport numbers;
- no birth dates, addresses, phone numbers or personal e-mail addresses;
- never names together with student or staff IDs.

Files that contain national ID numbers or birth dates are refused in the room.
